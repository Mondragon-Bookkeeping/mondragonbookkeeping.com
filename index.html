About the Firm
"The best tax work happens before the deadline. Most people find that out too late."

Mondragon Tax & Advisory is a tax & bookkeeping practice based in San Diego, California, serving small businesses and individuals across California and nationwide.

I work across two primary service areas. The first is ongoing monthly bookkeeping — financial close, reconciliations, payroll, and financial statement preparation — delivered as a year-round engagement for business owners who need consistent, reliable financial infrastructure. The second is advisory-level tax preparation and planning, available as a standalone service or as an add-on to any bookkeeping engagement. Every tax return includes a structured advisory review: findings, implications, and forward-looking recommendations tailored to each client's specific situation.

I work with a range of client types: owner-operated businesses in construction and the trades, cafés and restaurants whether finding their footing or scaling up, rental property owners managing single or multi-property portfolios, investors navigating capital gains and portfolio income, and solopreneurs and self-employed service providers building sustainable operations. The common thread is clients who need more than basic compliance — they need a practitioner who understands their numbers, communicates clearly, and stays engaged throughout the year.

Brandon Mondragon, founder and principal, manages all client work directly. Flat-rate pricing, a secure client portal, and consistent direct access are standard across every engagement.

Advisory-Level Tax Preparation

Your return, handled with care and precision

Every return includes a personalized advisory package — specific notes, findings, and action steps tailored to the client's situation. The goal is for every client to understand exactly what was filed and why, and what they can do differently going forward.

Details that affect the client's tax picture are surfaced and explained. Details that don't apply aren't mentioned. What every engagement delivers is a thorough, precise review — and a practitioner who follows through year-round.

How pricing works
Every return starts with a free consultation where we review your situation together. Pricing is based on the forms, schedules, and depth of work your return requires — the more moving parts, the more time and care it takes to do it right. A single W-2 return starts at $500. Your exact quote is provided before we begin, so there are no surprises.

What We Stand For

How the work
gets done.

The principles below shape every client engagement — from the first consultation to year-round advisory work.

01
Clarity over complexity
Tax and accounting are complex. Our job is to make sure you never feel that complexity. Every memo, every call — the most important thing is said first, and said plainly.
02
Precision without pretense
We triple-check everything. We own our mistakes. We say "I don't know, but I'll find out" instead of guessing. Accuracy matters more than the appearance of confidence.
03
We plan ahead
The best tax work happens before the deadline, not at it. We plan ahead, flag issues early, and make sure no client is ever blindsided by something we could have anticipated together.
04
Security by default
Sensitive information is exchanged through secure portals — never text, never open email. Client data is handled with the same care we bring to the work itself.
05
Access to real guidance
Good financial guidance should be precise, direct, and accessible — not an afterthought or a luxury. Every client engagement reflects that standard, regardless of the complexity of the work.
06
Always improving
Tax law changes. Technology changes. We change with it. We stay current, bring every relevant update to clients before they have to ask, and are always honest about what we're still learning.
Our mission
"Precise, proactive, and always looking ahead — that's the standard every client engagement is held to."
